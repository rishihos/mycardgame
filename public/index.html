    10 Code Card Game Rules

    1. 4 players, 2 teams (Seat 1 & 3 vs Seat 2 & 4, partners sit opposite).
    2. Each player is first dealt 5 cards. Everyone can see only their own 5 cards.
    3. The Power Colour Selector (room creator on the first game, or a member of the previous winning team on a rematch) secretly-to-others picks the Power Colour from their 5 cards.
    4. Once picked, the remaining 8 cards are dealt to everyone - all 13 cards are now visible in your hand. The Power Colour is shown publicly to the table.
    5. The Selector plays the first card. The winner of a trick leads the next trick.
    6. Players MUST follow the Lead Suit if they have it.
    7. If lacking the Lead Suit, you may play any card, including a Power Colour card.
    8. Highest Power Colour wins the trick. If none was played, the highest card of the Lead Suit wins (A is high, 2 is low).
    9. Winning Condition: There are only four 10s (10♠, 10♥, 10♣, 10♦). The first Team to capture 3 of the 4 wins instantly - the game doesn't need to go all 13 rounds.
    10. On a rematch in the same room, the winning team chooses the Power Colour and leads first next game.
